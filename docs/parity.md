# Parity with manuzio

melies must produce what manuzio's own crates (`mnz-audiobook`'s narration of a chapter, and
`mnz-trailer`) produced. The references in [crates/parity/references/](../crates/parity/references)
were recorded from those crates at the commit in [manuzio-source.md](manuzio-source.md), before any
code was copied, and the parity test ([crates/parity/tests/parity.rs](../crates/parity/tests/parity.rs))
checks melies against them. manuzio can run the same check after switching (see the end).

## What is recorded

The cases are defined once, in [cases.rs](../crates/parity/src/cases.rs), and run by
[run.rs](../crates/parity/src/run.rs) through an `Engine`: manuzio's crates in the harness, melies in
the parity test.

- **Inputs** (`inputs`): two stills (1200×1800 and 2400×1200, so the 9:16 frame crops them and the
  wide one pans) and five seconds of stereo Opus music, generated by the pinned FFmpeg
  ([fixtures.rs](../crates/parity/src/fixtures.rs), bit-exact encodes): no media is committed.
- **Narrations**, with Kokoro: five audiobook runs through manuzio's `create_audiobook`, each of
  edits of manuzio's sample book ([book.rs](../crates/parity/src/book.rs)):
  - `standard`: the Introduction and Chapter I, each cut to its first paragraph, `af_alloy`, the
    default settings;
  - `retail`: the same in the retail profile, with a closing pause of 7 s (kept to 5) and a sample
    rate of 22,050 Hz (made 44,100);
  - `labelled`: the same with the AI label, `bf_emma` at 1.0, other pauses and 48 kHz;
  - `ornaments`: the short Introduction with a scene break (`* * *`), Chapter I of a lone `⁂`, and
    Chapter II with no text (each of those two speaks only its heading);
  - `chinese`: two paragraphs of Chinese, Chapter I headed 第一章 龙卷风, `zf_xiaoxiao`.
- **Trailers**: four with a narrator that needs no model ([`tone`](../crates/parity/src/fixtures.rs),
  a sine as long as the text takes to read, so no scene is a whole number of frames): one scene
  without music or fades; three scenes with music, scene fades and music fades; three scenes
  without music or fades; two scenes with music and no fades. And one short trailer narrated by
  Kokoro, with music and music fades.

For each, kuleshov's recording session ([`kuleshov_parity::Session`](https://github.com/Federiker/kuleshov/blob/d3c0e5990b09d31b4d159d2c7632740181de7b34/crates/parity/src/session.rs))
keeps every `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` call with its arguments in order and the contents of the concat
lists among them, the result, the files returned (bytes, SHA-256, decoded audio `-f md5`, the
SHA-256 of the decoded video's `-f framemd5` listing, the stream parameters and tags) and the files
left in the case's folder. Paths, generated UUIDs and FFmpeg's log addresses are named as kuleshov
names them (`{work}`, `{uuid1}`, `{address}`).

melies' recording shim, [melies-record](../crates/parity/src/bin/melies-record.rs), is kuleshov's
([`kuleshov_parity::Shim`](https://github.com/Federiker/kuleshov/blob/d3c0e5990b09d31b4d159d2c7632740181de7b34/crates/parity/src/shim.rs))
with its `wavs` option: every WAV among the arguments, **or named in a concat list**, is recorded
by size and SHA-256 under the call's lists. That is how the chapter's WAV, which manuzio deletes
once the MP3 is encoded, is compared bit for bit.

Besides the runs, [inputs.json](../crates/parity/references/inputs.json) holds what manuzio read
from its sample book (SHA-256 recorded): each narration case's texts as manuzio's adapter will hand
them to melies (the spoken heading built as `plan_chapter` builds it, the subtitle, the body with a
line break after each paragraph, the notice before the first), the label's producer and content
identifier, and **the plans**: manuzio's `plan_chapter` (with the notice) of the sample book's
Introduction and Chapters I and II, whole, and of every text of the cases.

For the narrations, only the narration's own calls are kept: the harness drops the calls the retail
report makes on the finished files (`audiobook-parts`), which stay manuzio's, and both sides keep only
the MP3s, as `000.mp3`, `001.mp3`…, in the case's folder.

## How they are compared

- Plans: equal, piece for piece (kind, text and pause); melies' `Title` is manuzio's `Heading`.
- Runs: [`kuleshov_parity::compare`](https://github.com/Federiker/kuleshov/blob/d3c0e5990b09d31b4d159d2c7632740181de7b34/crates/parity/src/compare.rs):
  the same FFmpeg and FFprobe builds, the same calls (so the same WAV hashes), results, leftover
  files, and outputs equal byte for byte. The exception is room tone: FFmpeg's `anoisesrc` is seeded
  at random, so a retail MP3 differs from run to run even from manuzio itself; its calls, and so its
  WAV, must still be equal, and its stream parameters but the bit rate and duration.
- The Kokoro cases hold only on the machine the references were recorded on: Kokoro's decoder draws
  its noise in an order that follows ONNX Runtime's thread count, and ONNX Runtime's kernels differ by
  platform ([kempelen's parity notes](https://github.com/Federiker/kempelen/blob/b734f36ec02e01ae8d225952aca3aa52e085271a/docs/parity.md#the-machine)).
  Elsewhere their test says it is skipped and why.

Two recordings from manuzio, one after the other, agreed with each other completely (0 differences,
the retail cases' WAVs included), and so did both inputs files.

## The tests

- `the_plans_are_those_manuzio_made` and `the_trailers_are_those_manuzio_rendered` run in the light
  tier (`mise run test`): FFmpeg, no model.
- `the_narrations_and_the_kokoro_trailer_are_those_manuzio_made` is in the model tier
  (`mise run test:model`) and runs the whole set, Kokoro cases included, on the references' machine.

All passed at the first run after the port (27 October 2026), on Windows and on Linux (WSL), each on
its references' machine: the plans of all 17 texts, and all 11 runs, the five narrations' WAVs bit for
bit and the Kokoro trailer included. On Linux, too, two recordings from manuzio agreed completely, and
their inputs were byte for byte those recorded on Windows.

A change that alters a reference is a change of output: find out why first, and ask the owner before
recording new references. They are only ever recorded from manuzio, never from melies, and never
edited by hand.

## Recording them again

The harness is kept in the gitignored `tmp/harness` (owner's decision): to rebuild it, make a binary
crate with its own `[workspace]`, manuzio's `Cargo.lock` copied beside it, and path dependencies on
`../../../manuzio/crates/{audiobook,epub,lang,models,trailer,tools,audios,tts}` and
`../../crates/parity`. It implements `melies_parity::Engine` with manuzio's `create_audiobook`
(extracting the MP3s from the archive) and `render_trailer`/`render_with_narrator`, writes the texts
and plans as above, and runs `melies_parity::record` in a `Session` whose shim folder is
`MNZ_TOOLS_DIR` (with `MNZ_TTS_DATA_DIR` an empty folder and `MNZ_TTS_CUDA=off`, so manuzio reads its
own staged Kokoro files); a `compare A B` command compares two recordings.

```sh
cargo build -p melies-parity --bin melies-record
tmp/harness/target/debug/melies-harness target/debug/melies-record ../manuzio/assets/tools \
  ../manuzio/assets/samples/oz.epub tmp/work-harness crates/parity/references
```

Record twice and compare: nothing may differ.

On Linux, the harness and the shim are built natively in WSL (with a C toolchain and OpenSSL's
headers, `build-essential` and `libssl-dev`, which manuzio's crates need), with kuleshov's Linux pin
(`cargo run -p kuleshov --features download --example install_ffmpeg -- <folder>` installs it) and
manuzio's staged Kokoro files read through `/mnt/c`; the work folder on WSL's own disk.

## The check in manuzio

After switching, manuzio checks the commit it pins with its own staged FFmpeg and Kokoro files
(`assets/tools`, `assets/tools/tts`), from a checkout of melies at that commit:

```sh
MELIES_TOOLS_DIR=../manuzio/assets/tools MELIES_SPEECH_DIR=../manuzio/assets/tools/tts \
  cargo test -p melies-parity -- --include-ignored
```

and its own adapters with `melies-parity` as a dev-dependency at the same commit: for each
narration case, `melies_parity::write_book` makes the same edit of its sample book, its reader and
heading builder must give `Inputs::committed()`'s texts for that case, and its `BookTrailer` mapping
must give the trailer cases' settings (`TRAILERS`).
